### H6-class hypotheses: mechanism vs. condition confusion

When a hypothesis describes *how* a condition manifests (a propagation mechanism, a pathway) rather than *whether* it exists, it doesn't map cleanly to the MECE issue tree. These "mechanism hypotheses" need to be explicitly placed as sub-branches of a condition node (e.g., C2a under C2) rather than treated as standalone condition hypotheses. Watch for hypotheses of the form "X is the key mechanism by which Y happens" — these need a home in the tree, not a row of their own.

## Applied Learnings

- **Anonymise named internal advocates by default.** When a hypothesis or finding references a specific internal person's position, use a neutral role descriptor ("an internal advocate for X posture") not a name. Names in written analysis create political sensitivity.
- **All-unknown human review is a valid outcome.** If the human cannot unblock open blockers, the workplan is complete as a research artifact and the open questions are the deliverable. This is not a failure — it correctly identifies the information boundaries.
